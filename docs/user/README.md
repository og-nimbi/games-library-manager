## What the app does

- Scans supported storefront installations on your PC (currently Steam, itch.io, Epic Games)
- Builds a unified library view with covers and metadata when available
- Lets you launch games and storefront clients directly from the app
- Optionally creates provider folders and shortcuts in your chosen library root
- Caches artwork and saves your preferences locally

## What the app does not do

- It does not install games, storefront clients, emulators, or runtimes
- It does not bypass DRM or licensing requirements
- It does not require an account or upload your library by default
